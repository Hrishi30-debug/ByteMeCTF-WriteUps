Hii,

Today I went for a trek.

I live in Nigdi, Pradhikaran, so I walked to the station and took a local from there.

After 6 stops our destination was on the 7th station.

Got off there. The fort wasn't exactly near the station — Maps showed something around 20–22 km away.

From there we took a bus and got down somewhere near the base. Had to walk another 1–2 km after that.

The fort was around 3,500 ft high. It looked almost like a pyramid from a distance.

It took us about half an hour to climb. We basically raced to the top.

Then we rested for a while and took some photos.

From the top we could see two other forts. I got some nice photos of them and marked them 1 and 2 because I didn't know their names.

On the way back, we stopped beside a lake. From there, another fort was visible across the water. It looked quite different from what we saw from the top.

I didn't write down the names. I'll figure them out later.

The trip was worth it. I will give it great review later.
